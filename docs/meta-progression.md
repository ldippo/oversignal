# OVERSIGNAL — Meta Progression v2 (HEAT / GROOVE / RULES)

Locked 2026-08-27 (grill session). Problem: builds read as stat sheets; speed,
score, and scrap were barely linked; defense was just a bigger bar.

## HEAT — the earning engine

A visible tier multiplier ×1…×5 applied to ALL score and scrap gains.

- **Builds from:** sustained >85% top speed · near-misses · PERFECT gates
- **Drops from:** any damage = −1 full tier · braking/coasting = slow bleed
- **Near-miss:** crossing a shard/barrier un-hit within 2.5m lateral gap =
  +25% of a tier, amber spark burst, "CLOSE" float text, zing sfx. Generous
  and loud so it never feels random.
- **HUD:** heat bar + ×N glow beside the score.
- **Economy rebase:** baseline earn rates ×0.6 of today. Lane-campers earn
  slightly less than before; flow-state runs earn 3–4× more. Applies in daily
  mode too (layout stays deterministic; heat is pure skill expression).

## GROOVE — defense as behavior

- **Groove streak:** alive while consecutive on-beat gates / unbroken ring
  chains continue; breaks on a missed gate or any hit.
- **Groove regen:** hull regenerates (+2/s base) ONLY while the streak is
  alive. Ship aura glow while regenerating. Sustain builds must play musically.
- **Typed mitigation** (two separate stats, set by ships/modules):
  - *scrape armor* — wall-grind damage ×0…×1
  - *impact armor* — shard/barrier/fence damage ×0.5…×1

Owned stale modules (FOURTH PIP, LONG DROP, OD CHARGER, MAGNET+, RICH RINGS,
CHAIN KEEPER) convert to nearest equivalents or refund. Synergy draft cards
re-aim at the new families.

## Rollout

One pass: heat + groove + mitigation + ship rules + module rework + economy
rebase in a single deploy — one coherent balance moment. No classic-mode
toggle.
